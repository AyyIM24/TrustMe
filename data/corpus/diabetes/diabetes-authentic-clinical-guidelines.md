# Diabetes & Metabolic (ICMR-INDIAB Scope) — Authentic Clinical Guidelines & Verified Evidence

The landmark ICMR-INDIAB study revealed that over 101 million Indians live with diabetes and 136 million with prediabetes. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

Type 1 diabetes is an autoimmune condition where beta cells are destroyed, requiring lifelong exogenous insulin to survive. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

Type 2 diabetes is a progressive metabolic condition combining insulin resistance with relative insulin deficiency. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

Drinking Karela (bitter gourd) juice, Jamun seed powder, or Methi water cannot cure diabetes or substitute for prescribed insulin. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

Abruptly discontinuing prescribed insulin or metformin in favor of unverified herbal churna can cause fatal Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA). Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

Diabetes is not caused solely by consuming white sugar; genetics, abdominal visceral adiposity, and physical inactivity play major roles. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

People living with diabetes can safely eat whole low-glycemic fruits in controlled moderation as part of a balanced diet. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

HbA1c tests measure average blood glucose over 90 days; target levels should be monitored under a certified physician's care. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.
