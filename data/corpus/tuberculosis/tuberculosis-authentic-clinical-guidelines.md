# Tuberculosis (India NTEP Scope) — Authentic Clinical Guidelines & Verified Evidence

Tuberculosis is an infectious airborne bacterial disease caused by Mycobacterium tuberculosis, not a genetic disorder. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

India carries the highest global TB burden, and the Government of India provides free diagnosis and DOTS drugs via Ni-kshay. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

TB is 100% curable when the full 6-to-9 month course of first-line antibiotics (Rifampicin, Isoniazid, etc.) is completed. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

Stopping TB medication midway or relying on herbal remedies leads to life-threatening Multi-Drug Resistant TB (MDR-TB). Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

Tuberculosis cannot be cured by raw garlic, turmeric, unproven homoeopathy, or dietary tonics alone. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

TB is spread through airborne droplets when an infected person coughs, sneezes, or spits; it does NOT spread by sharing food. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

BCG vaccination given at birth protects Indian children from severe complications like tubercular meningitis. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

A persistent cough lasting more than 2 weeks with fever and weight loss requires immediate sputum examination (CBNAAT/Truenat). Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.
