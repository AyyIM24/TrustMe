Prevent mosquito bites
Dengue is spread to people through the bite of an infected mosquito. Mosquitoes bite during the day and night. There is no widely available dengue vaccine to prevent dengue infection. The best way to prevent dengue is to protect yourself from mosquito bites.
Use insect repellent.
Wear loose-fitting, long-sleeved shirts and pants.
Take steps to control mosquitoes in and around your home.

Currently, there are no dengue vaccines available for use in the United States.
In 2021, a dengue vaccine, Dengvaxia, was approved for use in children aged 9–16 years with laboratory-confirmed previous dengue virus infection and living in areas where dengue is common.
In 2024, Sanofi-Pasteur, Dengvaxia's manufacturer, discontinued vaccine production because of a lack of demand in the global market.
There are other dengue vaccines either approved or in late stages of development in other countries. However, they are not currently available in the United States.
