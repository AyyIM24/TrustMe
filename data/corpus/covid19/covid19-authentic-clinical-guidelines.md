Approved COVID-19 vaccines do not alter, integrate into, or modify human DNA. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

Coronil, herbal kadhas, and unverified traditional supplements cannot cure acute COVID-19 or replace oxygen therapy. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

Drinking hot water, inhaling steam, or consuming raw garlic does not kill coronavirus inside infected lung tissue. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

Wearing three-ply or N95 masks significantly limits aerosol transmission in indoor and crowded settings. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

5G cellular towers and electromagnetic waves do not transmit or generate the biological SARS-CoV-2 virus. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

Ivermectin and hydroxychloroquine were removed from MoHFW clinical guidelines due to lack of randomized efficacy. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.

Severe COVID-19 can cause acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS) requiring medical oxygen and corticosteroids. Patients must follow certified medical care and verified protocols.
